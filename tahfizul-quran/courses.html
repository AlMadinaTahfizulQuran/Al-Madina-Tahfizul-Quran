Explore Our Qur'an Courses

Find the perfect course for your learning journey, whether you're a beginner, intermediate learner, or looking to refresh your skills.

COURSE 01

Qur’an Course for Children

A fun and engaging introduction to the Qur’an for young learners.

Our children’s course is designed to make learning the Qur’an enjoyable and easy to follow. Through interactive one-to-one online classes, children learn from the basics, develop correct pronunciation and build a love for the Qur’an in a supportive environment.

  • Learn the Arabic alphabet
  • Develop correct pronunciation (Tajweed)
  • Interactive and child-friendly lessons
  • One-to-one online classes
  • Supportive and encouraging teachers
  • Build a strong foundation

COURSE 02

Qur’an Course for Adults

Structured learning for adults at all levels.

Our adult course is suitable for anyone who wishes to learn to read the Qur’an, improve their recitation or strengthen their understanding. Lessons are tailored to your level and goals, with flexible timings to fit around your lifestyle.

  • Suitable for beginners and intermediate learners
  • Improve recitation and understanding
  • One-to-one online classes
  • Learn at your own pace
  • Flexible scheduling
  • Support from experienced teachers

COURSE 03

Qur’an Memorisation Course

A dedicated programme for those who wish to memorise the Qur’an.

Our memorisation course (Hifz) is designed to support students in their journey to memorise the Qur’an, with structured lesson plans, regular revision and guidance from experienced teachers.

  • Structured memorisation programme
  • Regular revision and assessment
  • One-to-one online classes
  • Personalised learning plan
  • Guidance from experienced teachers
  • Ongoing support and motivation

COURSE 04

Qur’an Refresher Course

Revise, strengthen and reconnect with the Qur’an.

This course is for those who already have some knowledge of the Qur’an and wish to revise what they have learned, improve their recitation or strengthen their previously memorised portions.

  • Revise previous learning
  • Improve recitation and fluency
  • Identify and correct mistakes
  • Flexible and personalised lessons
  • One-to-one online classes
  • Support from experienced teachers
